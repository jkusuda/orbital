About Orbital

A 3D view of the asteroids passing close to Earth today, shown where they actually are right now. Drag the time slider to see where they were or will be up to a week either way, and click an asteroid for details.

  • NASA's NEO API lists today's close approaches and the orbit of each asteroid.
  • Go uses those orbits to calculate where each asteroid is, relative to Earth, at any moment.
  • Three.js draws them in your browser. Distances are compressed and sizes exaggerated so everything fits on screen.
